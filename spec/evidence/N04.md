# N04 Weapon assistance (companion firing)
## Behaviour
An armed AI companion in the assist or shoot states fires its own weapon at the target chosen by its vision/serveur code (`this+0xc60`). It uses exactly Jack's weapon tables, hitscan and damage function; its ammo is its own slot in the same arrays (`G+0x342c+0x48*w+4*slot` clip, `G+0x39d4+...` reserve). It will not shoot at some enemy kinds, stops when the target is too far (half the weapon's ray length), and backs out when the target is closer than that kind's safe distance.

- `H_exec_validate_tir@0x5ad900`: fires only when the cooldown `this+0x1124 -= dt` reached 0; weapon in hand has ammo (clip or reserve) or is a throwable (6,7,8); target kind not 0x1d/0x1e; `this+0xbf4 == 0` (weapon not lowered); a line of sight (`fn@0x6e48c0`) or state assist; facing: `dot(forward, dir to target) >= 0.5`; on success it switches to the shoot state (`DAT_00b99330`). When the cooldown reaches 0 without a usable weapon it is reset to 1.0 [C].
- `H_ETAT_IA_help@0x5b1720` (0x25f, assist order 1): approaches the order target to 5.0 m (2.0 for the special target `G2+0x10d8`), engages at half the weapon ray (shotgun 8.0; 100 when `this+0x3364 == 0` and it holds a non-firearm item [L]), gives up after 30.0 s (`this+0x3d48`) [C].
- `H_callback_tir@0x5bbd10`: AI gate `v_cible_arme (this+0x182c) != 0` and aim-ready `this[0x60c] >= 0.95`; sets `this[0x30a] = 0.2` for the human; damage through `fn@0x53a370` (`Weapon_DamageAtDistance`) exactly as for Jack, so a companion bullet hurts an enemy as much as Jack's [C by absence of a companion branch].
- Friendly fire: a hit on a companion whose source is the player is dropped unless `this[0x2a9]` is set (`H_exec_ch_Stimulus_Paf`) [C].
