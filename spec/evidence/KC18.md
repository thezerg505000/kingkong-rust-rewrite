# KC18 Defensive positioning
Main documents: `spec/evidence/KC09.md` (dodge), `spec/evidence/KC01.md`, `spec/evidence/KF01.md` (fury resilience).

## Behaviour (what the game does)
Survival against a big enemy is built from: (1) dodge i-frames (side-step anims 6/7, counter lunge 0x16), (2) hit-stun rules, (3) fury resilience, (4) the last-stand rule.
**Receiver** (`k_exec_detect_paf@0x8c3fc0`, per frame, every hit of the frame is scanned; only the **largest damage** hit of the frame is applied
(`Life_ApplyDamage(Kong+0x1aa0, damage)`), flags in `Kong+0xc74`): 0x20 ignored; 0x400 plays anim 0x42 without damage; 0x80 "repeated" hits count in a 4.0 s window and
the fourth (or any during mashing, action 0x160) deals **30.0**; 0x10000 (grab) only sets the hit vector; a hit with flag 4 does not interrupt;
the normal hit also sets the red hit flash (`Kong+0x1fec = 1.0`, blend 5/s toward `Kong+0x1ff0..0x1ffc`) and knockback `Kong+0x1ef0 = 0.75`. If last stand is armed
(`Kong+0x1fc8`) and the hit interrupts: with `Kong+0x1fcc < 5.0` Kong dies (`Kong+0x1e38 = 0x1f`), otherwise the timer is set to 5.0 (KF05).
**Hit stun** (`k_ETAT_paf@0x8c24a0`, action 600): heavy hits (flag 2) pick anims 0x2c-0x2f, light (0x81) 0x28-0x2b/0x29, flag 4 pushes with anim 0x27; the stun ends at
the animation end (return to `k_ETAT_main`, or `k_ETAT_main_souche` while holding). In light stun (anims 0x28/0x2b) an attack press after frame 0xf cancels into a punch
(`fn@0x884b00(10)`). Between 0.25 s and 0.7 s (0.4 s for anim 0x29) a knocked-back Kong hurts what he hits for 10. Fury: each hit taken cuts 2.5/5.0 s of fury (KF01).
**Obstacles**: dodge/roll and climb share one button (KC09); enemies cannot be dodged when 2+ riders are on Kong.
