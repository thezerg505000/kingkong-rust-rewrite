# G17 Nonlethal distraction (Jack versus the V-Rex)
Main doc: `X04.md` (section "Jack level"). Species 0x10 of `PNJ_Raptor_*`.

## Behaviour (what the game does)
Jack cannot hurt the rex and cannot kill it with any normal weapon: `exec_check_paf@0x849910` never subtracts hp for species 0x10. Gunfire still matters in four ways:
1. **Noise**: every shot creates a noise event of radius 150 m (`H_callback_tir@0x5bbd10` -> `fn@0x6e3d30(... 0x43160000, flags 0x2000)`); `exec_check_sound@0x8478d0` flags the shooter 0x800|0x4 for any rex inside 150 m. A heard-only Jack is picked up again after the rex has lost him for 1.5 s (rule "wound ratio" of `update_best_interest`). [C]
2. **Shots that pass near** (closest approach < 8 m) flag the shooter 0x2 (`exec_check_shoot@0x85b0a0`). [C]
3. **Hits**: every hit adds its damage to a per-attacker ledger (`+0x1cdc[i]`). Once an attacker has done **more than 10** damage (or a single hit carries paf bit 0x400, or the hit has zero damage) its slot gets `0x20` (+`0x1` when the attacker is Jack). Rule 3 of the target chain then makes Jack the target (even over a corpse); rule 4 switches to any 0x20 attacker after 4 s on the current target. [C]
4. **Reaction**: if Jack is within 20 m (or can be reached in <= 3.5 s) a hit slows the rex by 0.5 m/s (hit < 10) or 5.0 m/s (>= 10), only if it was moving (`+0x1c30`); a single hit of >= 20 damage (sniper, close shotgun) requests a target re-think (`+0x1440 = 1` -> HESITE: the rex turns, roars after 0.5-0.8 s, then resumes). There is no flinch/hurt animation state for the rex. [C]
So the answer to "attracts or interrupts?": it attracts (noise, shot-near, ledger) and interrupts only through the HESITE re-think and the short speed loss. It never loses interest because of damage.

## Code
`exec_check_paf@0x849910` (species 0x10 branch), `exec_check_sound@0x8478d0`, `exec_check_shoot@0x85b0a0`, `exec_update_best_interest@0x863360` (rules 3, 4, wound-ratio rule), `ETAT_HESITE@0x864c30`, `fn@0x487910` (intercept time: 0 inside 20 m, else distance / closing speed, 1e14 if opening).

## Numbers
noise radius 150 m [C]; shot-near 8 m (squared compare with 64) [C]; ledger threshold 10 [C]; "hurt me" retarget after 4.0 s [C]; hit slow 0.5 / 5.0 m/s, split at 10 [C]; re-think hit >= 20 [C]; react window 20 m / 3.5 s [C]; Jack's weapon damage 1..20 per hit by range (`Weapon_DamageAtDistance@0x53a370`, 1000 with the one-shot cheat bit 0xbf0&2 -> still no hp change) [C].

## Data
Weapon flag word of a bullet paf: bits 0x40|0x4 (`H_callback_tir` passes `0x44` to `fn@0x6e3e70`) [C, decompile of the call is stack-garbled; `L` for the exact value].

## Gaps
The re-think request has more triggers than the 20-damage rule ported in `on_hit`: a normal (non-0x400) hit on hit-zone 0, 1 or 0xf5 (the head bone) while the rex is in MORD, or while `+0x38b8` is set and the attacker is Jack, a 0x400 hit by Jack at most every 5 s, or a hit that brings the speed to 0 (`check_paf` lines around `local_98 = 1`); zone ids are not decoded. Which scripted source sends the exact flag words 0x200/0x100/0x80 to the rex (a `TrigExec_SendPaf` in the level, not found in 03E data); sound radius of weapons other than the three `H_callback_tir` cases (spear throws, thrown bait) not read; the ledger reset (`+0x1c40` is zeroed on entering DEVORE only).
