# X18 Skull Island natives (`PNJInd_*`, `Na_*`, `KNa_*`, `KNG_*`, `KWC_*`)
Status RESEARCHED.

## Behaviour
Four native families. (1) `PNJInd_*` (14 functions `@0x69c560-0x6a0f70`): Jack-level spear natives that `Hide` / `Hidden` in cover, come `GoOut`, `Attack` (charge `ChargeAttack`, `Exec_Weapon` spear), take hits (`Paf`, `PafMaster`), die (`Mort`); `Exec_MoveToInit` returns them home. (2) `Na_*` (16 functions `@0x4e7990-0x4ed770`): spear throwers on elevated structures: wait for activation (`WaitActivation`), move (`Move`, `GotoPoint`), `Launch`/`Exec_Lance` a thrown spear, scripted (`Exec_Script`). (3) `KNa_*` (17 functions `@0x55b190-0x5602e1`): Kong-level natives (same plus `Follow_Network`, `Borg`). (4) `KNG_*` (13 functions `@0x4c1180-0x4c6210`) native group spawner/controller (`Wait`, `Movement`, `Digwazai`, `Reload`, `SpawnNative`, `Destroy`) and `KWC_*` (6 functions `@0x74f5e0-0x750330`: `Active_Bhv`, `Attack_Exec`, `WazaiYell`, `DeactivateNatives`) the war-chief that yells and activates/deactivates the natives. `KPluieDeLances_*`/`PluieDeLances_*` are the volley effects ("rain of spears").
