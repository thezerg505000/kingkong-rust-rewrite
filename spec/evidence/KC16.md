# KC16 Environmental kills
Main documents: `spec/evidence/KC07.md`, `spec/evidence/KC14.md`; fire is E-group.

## Behaviour (what the game does)
No Kong-specific "hazard kill" function exists. Kills happen through ordinary damage paths: a thrown creature takes impact damage on any wall/ground
contact (rex 20.0, raptor 5.0, Scolo 5.0), thrown objects (KIGO) damage whatever they hit (class 2/0x22, `KIGO+0x18`), flagged hits kill outright
(class 8 downward and class 0x20 set a Kong-level raptor's life to 0), and creatures also die when they fall (the `*_ETAT_chute`/`Chute_Libre` states).
Fire/water hazards are level objects handled by the environment group (E-IDs, `Fire_PointInFireZone`, `Interactive_*`), not by Kong's code.
So "throw into a hazard" is the combination of KC07 + a hazard volume; no Kong code was found that targets hazards.
