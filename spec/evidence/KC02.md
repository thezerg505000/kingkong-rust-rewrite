# KC02 Repeated attacks
Main document: `spec/evidence/KC01.md` (input graph and blow table).

## Behaviour (what the game does)
The ground combo has exactly two chained punches: attack, attack (phase 1 -> 2 -> end). A third attack press after
the second does **not** punch again; it only works after a repel (special) when the downward strike (phase 5)
follows, and the downward strike's attack press restarts the chain at punch 1. So the longest ground chain is
punch, punch, repel, downward, punch, ... Pressing attack in the idle/recovery phase starts punch 1 again.
Holding a weapon uses its own alternating swing chain (KC14).

## Code
`k_ETAT_main@0x89a120` phase handlers 1 (0x89aaba), 2 (0x89aeb4), 3 (0x89b2aa), 5 (0x89bd5d), 9 (0x89e634): attack latched
-> `fn@0x884d40` / `fn@0x885010` / `fn@0x884de0` / `fn@0x884b00(0)`. The "third attack" in the fury sequence
(KF01) is the special button during the repel, not a third punch.
