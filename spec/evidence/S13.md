# S13 Fire damage
## Behaviour (what the game does)
A burning spear adds 10 to the damage of its hit (dev spear 11 -> 21 within 10 m, bone 7 -> 17, far 5 -> 15) and the melee message carries flag 0x40 instead of 0x10 (the target's reaction, e.g. `PNJ_Raptor_ETAT_BURN`, is the receiver's job).

## Code
`Javelin_launch` `local_fc + 10` when `this[0x3e] != 0` [C]; `H_exec_test_ZDE_FIGHT` flag 0x40 when `G+0x4520+4p == 1` [C]; damage-over-time on the victim is in the species state (BURN states), not in the spear.

## Rust
`BURN_DAMAGE_BONUS`, `Spear::hit_damage`, `melee_flag`; tests `spear_rows_in_flight_use_weapon_table`, `melee_constants`.

## Gaps
Burn tick damage and duration on creatures; damage to flammable scenery (grid fire, `ode_exec_gestion_feu`) not researched.
