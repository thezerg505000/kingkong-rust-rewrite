# X04 Adult V-Rex (Jack side and Kong side)
Two different machines share the name "V-Rex":
* **Kong side**: `KT_*` (`PNJ_KTREX@0x4983b0`), a life-gauge brawler fighting Kong and Ann. Full recovery in `B02.md`; summary in "Kong level" below.
* **Jack side**: species byte `0x10` of the shared `PNJ_Raptor_*` class (the same class that runs the Venatosaurus 0xe and the compies 0x16, see `X02.md`).
  It is **not** a KT instance and **not** the `TrigExec_*` functions: the old note "PNJ_Tyranosaure_Jack" is not in the name table; the Jack-level rex
  is `PNJ_Raptor` with `this+0x20 == 0x10` (`PNJ_Raptor_init@0x8315a0` branch `iVar3 == 0x10`). Level 03E stores it as `raptor_type 16`
  with an `NET_TRex` waypoint network (`fx/rex_encounter_03e.json`). [C]
Tags: `[C]` read from decompiled code of `KingKong8.exe` (function@address cited), `[L]` inferred, `[G]` guess.

# Jack level

## Behaviour (what the game does)
The rex is a 2000-hp creature that Jack's weapons **cannot hurt at all**: the damage routine `check_paf@0x849910` has no hp subtraction for species 0x10
(`fn@0x47cdd0` is called only in the 0xe and 0x16 branches). Only three exact paf flag words cap its hp (0x200 = 0, 0x100 = 15 %, 0x80 = 30 %), and those
come from level scripts, not from Jack's guns. [C]

It patrols the waypoint network (territory) of its level. It perceives Jack in a ~261 degree cone out to 100 m with line of sight, hears every gunshot within
150 m, and notices any shot passing within 8 m. Anything non-human (raptors, corpses, plants) is "seen" with no line of sight. Perceived actors go into a
20-slot list (`fn@0x47e180`); a fixed priority chain (`update_best_interest@0x863360`) picks the target. A new target sends it through HESITE: it turns to
face the target (blend 4*dt) and after a random 0.5-0.8 s plays a **roar** (camera shake within 25 m). Then FIGHT: it chases at walk speed 2.2 m/s,
or at the run gait (14 m/s, 17 m/s if Jack is attached to something) when the target is Jack; speed rises by only 1.5 m/s per second, so it needs ~9 s to reach full
run speed unless a `TrigExec_RexChaseRange` raised the acceleration for a frame. At the bite range it bites. A bite on a **healthy** Jack
(life ratio > 0.15) is a paf of damage 1000 with flags 0x4104 (not a grab; Jack's receiver `H_exec_ch_Stimulus_Paf@0x5a3060` never reads the damage value, only the flags: bit 4 selects the longest wound timer 15.5/13.5/10.5 s [C], so this is a **wound** [L]); a bite on a **wounded** Jack
(ratio 0.15), or any approach closer than 2.5 m (`hard_grab`), starts the **grab**: the rex holds Jack and at the end of the hold sends a second paf of 1000 with flags
0x4a10 (contains the 0x200 "dead" bit) = death. Shooting it never kills it; it makes Jack the preferred target once he has done more than 10 damage, and a
single hit of 20 or more makes the rex stop and re-think (roar). It loses interest when the target has not been seen for 1.5 s (SEARCH: walk to the last
position, wander a 20 m ring), when its 20 s target commitment lapses and something scores higher, when Jack hides (script mode 2 with hide sub-flag), or
when a corpse is nearer (it eats: 4 meat points per second).

### Same AI as the raptor?
Yes: one class, one state table, one target chain; the species byte selects constants and a handful of branches. Differences that matter (all `[C]`):
* no hp loss from bullets (raptor: `Raptor_ApplyDamage`), no PAF_SLIDE/FALL/FLY flinch states from bullets (those flags 0x1c18/0x1c1c/0x1c24 are only set in the 0xe/0x16 branches);
* `check_requin` (shark/wait after a bite) returns at once for the rex and clears `+0x1aa4`, so there is no post-bite wait;
* target scoring zeroes candidates outside the rex's territory mask `+0x8f0` (`fn@0x470690`) unless `+0x3d0` (attack Jack anywhere) and the slot is Jack;
* it re-thinks the target every 20 s (`fn@0x43c690(+0x1458, 20.0)` -> weight 0);
* the bite is 1000 damage 0x4104 / grab-kill 0x4a10, not 6-10 damage;
* it only sprints after Jack (`+0x1a58 = 1` iff target is Jack or has an attach parent), other prey is chased at walk speed;
* once alerted (`+0x1450 = 1`, set the first time it acquires a target) it considers every slot, seen or not.

### Level-side trigger parameters
`TrigExec_RexChaseRange@0x78b900(trigger)`: `target AI instance = *(*(*(trigger+4)+0x18)+4)+0x40`; writes `+0x38a8 = 1`, `+0x38ac = trigger[+0x18]` (float),
`+0x38b0 = trigger[+0x24]`. [C] `select_action` (species 0x10 block) reads `+0x38a8` / `+0x38ac`: when a target exists and the rex is moving, the override flag is cleared and `+0x38ac`
replaces the acceleration (1.5) **for that one speed update**; the target speed is then the run-gait value as usual. So "RexChaseRange" is an
**acceleration kick**, not a chase radius, and must be re-sent every frame to have a lasting effect (the trigger sits in a volume that fires each frame while inside [L]). `+0x38b0`
is written but no reader exists in `PNJ_Raptor_*` [C, gap]. `TrigExec_KTREX_Charge@0x78bd60` is the **Kong-level** trigger (sends AI message 9 to a KT rex = charge target, see B02).

# Kong level (summary; full detail in `B02.md`)
## Behaviour
In the Kong levels the V-Rex (`KT_*`) fights Kong and Ann, not Jack. It is a long-range brawler: sweeps (<= 8), jaw lunges (5.5..14), charges (>= 10-20 away), stomps and head-butts,
grabs Kong in its jaws (starting the GrabKong mash), can finish a wounded Kong, and answers Kong's fury shout with a roar. Full state machine, damage rules and numbers: `spec/evidence/B02.md`.
## Code
`PNJ_KTREX@0x4983b0`, `KT_TRACK_init@0x498640`, `KT_TRACK_reflex@0x49a430`, `KT_ETAT_*` (see B02). Level trigger `TrigExec_KTREX_Charge@0x78bd60` (message 9 = charge target).
## Numbers
Rex attack packets to Kong: jaw 10 (flags 2), head-butt 10 (1), charge 10 (0x42), flung-rex body 20 (0x41), sweep 10 (1), tail 10 (2) [C, `KT_exec_zdf_zdc@0x4a5f00`]. Life gauge values are level data.
