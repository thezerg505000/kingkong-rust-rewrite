# S08 Spear recovery
## Behaviour (what the game does)
A spear lying in the world (stuck in the ground/a creature or on the floor) can be taken again. Within reach (3 m, 4 m while walking straight at it) the spear shows a pickup highlight; pressing the interaction button
sets flag 0x8000 on the javelin, which writes the pickup mailbox in the Univers, consumed by Jack's tracker. Spears in water cannot be picked up. Spears more than 15 m from Jack are removed after 3 s.

## Code
- `Javelin_waittaken`@0x87d160 (called from `Javelin_Plug` and `Javelin_endofmove`): highlight animation index (`(*(gao+0x18)+8)+4`) = 9 when `local_2c^2 >= |v|^2` else 0; `local_2c = 3.0 (+1.0 if dot(dir, Jack axis) > 0.9 and |Jack speed| > 1.0)` [C];
  0 when Jack already holds the same weapon id, or the spear is a bone spear (7) while Jack holds weapon 6 [C]; not offered at all when `this[1]==3`, `this[0x87]!=0` or the ground at the spear has flag 0x40 (water) [C].
  On `fn@0x415a60(self,0x8000)`: `G+0x456c = weapon id (6/7/8)`, `G+0x4570 = Jack`, `G+0x4574 = this[0x3e]` (burning), `G+0x4578 = this[0x86]` (wear), `G+0x457c = 1`; spear goes to its destroy state [C].
  Despawn: `Obj_DistSq(spear, player) > 225.0` (15 m) or `this[1]==3` or water: `this[0x85] += dt`; `> 3.0` -> `fn@0x412440` (destroy), except when the spear is the newest (`Univers+0x1e1c`) [C].
- `H_TRACK_Reflex`@0x58de70 lines ~541-655 consumes the mailbox when `fn@0x68db30()==0` (not busy) and (NPC or Jack in state 0x23/0x22 with anim ended): see I05/I06 for the slot update.
- Item boxes (world pickups that are not thrown spears): `ib_wait`@0x6ef900 type 0/1/2 -> weapon 6/7/8 (box stays until `G+0x4570` cleared, `ib_waitdestroy`@0x6f0590).
- NPC-only proposal `H_exec_propose_ramasse`@0x5a2250 (the whole function is guarded by `this[0]==0`): range 5.0 (18.0 in a few NPC states), refused when holding a throwable or a loaded gun.
