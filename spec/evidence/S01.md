## Behaviour (what the game does)
Weapon id 6 "Javelin" (the only spear named in the exe: `GG_Exec_AppendWeaponName`@0x696240). Javelin type 0. Damage 11 within 10 m of the throw point, 5 beyond; 6 wear budget (3 embeds); melee uses the same item (stab animation 0x5f).
