# X07 Giant crab (`CR_*` large, `C_*` small, `PNJ_Crab`)
Status RESEARCHED.

## Behaviour
Two crab classes. `CR_*` (30 functions `@0x4889b0-0x7291d6`, init `@0x710580`) is the combat crab: it spawns from the water edge (`APPARITION`), idles on a network (`IDLE`), fights (`FIGHT`: target, corpse, return to den, search, strings `"vague d'attaque"` = attack wave, `"a atteint son antre"` = reached its den), grabs prey (`GRAB`), eats (`MANGE`), drags (`TRAINE`), takes hits (`PAF`), dies (`MORT`); `ETAT_TEST_GRILLE` tests grid cells. Crabs attack in waves (`TrigExec_CRAB_SetVagueNb`, `TrigExec_Spawner_CrabsTransfertGao`; level "01B Crabs"); large ones can damage structures [L: ledger text]. `C_*` (9 functions `@0x774040`) is the small crab prey object: `Not_Grabbed` / `Grabbed` states, can be plugged on a spear (`C_callback_pluglance`: spear within 2 m, `dist^2 < 4`), publishes a beacon (weight 100 when not carried).
