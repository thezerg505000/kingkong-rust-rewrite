# KF04 Increased damage
## Behaviour (what the game does)
Kong's basic melee blow does 10 damage and is a light hit (class 1). With fury it does 20 and is a heavy
hit (class 2), which the V-Rex counts as knock-down capable (KC10).
## Code
`fn@0x88e0c0@0x88e0c0` (Kong melee hit, called from the attack states): three sites
(0x88ea37, 0x88eb9e, 0x88f07f) do `fild 10; (fury) fadd 10.0 (_DAT_00a88e24); _ftol`; the third also sets the
hit class `local_c = 2` (fury) or `1`. The damage is passed to the hit-landed hook `fn@0x883050(.., class,
.., damage, ..)`, which builds the paf packet (`fn@0x6e3e70`) the victim reads with `fn@0x51f110`.
## Numbers
10 -> 20 damage; class 1 -> 2 [C].
## Data
Other attack damages (grab, jump, ground-pound) come from the grab/jump states; not audited.
## Rust
`kong::fury::{BASE_HIT_DAMAGE, FURY_DAMAGE_BONUS, Fury::melee_damage, melee_hit_class}`.
