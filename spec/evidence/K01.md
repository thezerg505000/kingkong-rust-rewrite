# K01 Third-person movement (main document; K03, K05, K11 point here)
Tags: `[C]` visible in the decompiled C, `[L]` inferred, `[G]` guess. Offsets are `Kong+0xNNN`. Static analysis of KingKong8.exe only.

## Behaviour
The left stick is read, negated, rotated by the active camera's axes, flattened to the ground and normalised; its length becomes an analogue 0..1 value. Kong has no walk-speed constants: the ground speed comes from the animation that the main state picks (anim id at `Kong+0x112c`, handler chosen by a jump table in `k_ETAT_main`), so heavier gaits simply play different clips. A direction latch stops a sudden camera cut from spinning Kong around: after the camera forward axis turns more than 45 degrees, the old world direction is kept until the stick direction differs by more than ~26 degrees (dot < 0.9) or 0.5 s pass with the secondary button held.
