# E08 Wooden barricades
## Behaviour
`Interactive_Obstacle` (AI model bound by `Interactive_Obstacle@0x7a37e0`, states `IOB_Etat_Init@0x7a3820`, `IOB_Etat_Go@0x7a3c40`, `IOB_CallBack_Destroy@0x7a3bf0`, `IOB_TAG_OFF@0x7a4020`) is a barricade built of up to 10 pieces. Each piece has a damage threshold. Hits on the barricade produce damage points; when the accumulated damage reaches a piece's threshold that piece is released as physics debris with an impulse; when every piece is gone the barricade plays its break animation, loses its interaction/collision, sends a screen effect message, and the debris fades after 6.0 s. A very strong hit destroys all pieces at once.
## Code
- `fn@0x649200@0x649200` (named here `IOB_exec_collect_damage`): scans the hit events of the frame. For each: paf flag 0x10 -> 1 damage point; else hit strength `fn@0x51f110` > resistance `this+8` (float) -> 100 points; else weak-hit counter `this+0x8c*4` ++, the 6th weak hit gives 1 point and resets. At most one result per frame; pieces below threshold get a small shake (`this+0x1a8+4i = Rand(0.005..0.02)`, jitter applied by `fn@0x6499b0` every frame, decaying with `t=clamp(10*dt)`) [C].
- `IOB_Etat_Go`: per piece i: if `damage + points < threshold_i` -> shake, not done; if `threshold_i <= damage` already gone; else release: debris timer `this+0x200+4i = 6.0`, physics on (`fn@0x435d80`), kick `fn@0x6494d0(piece, dir, points)` with magnitude `this+4` (points < 3) or `this+0xc` (>= 3) and +-0.5 rad random yaw, mass param 100.0. `damage += points`. When all pieces released: anim 1, clear interaction flag 0x8000, `Msg_SendJackScreenFx(code 0xff)`, sound id `this+0x234` via the sound table, `IOB_TAG_OFF` (removes the navigation-cut "tag" that blocked the network) [C].
- `fn@0x649630` (debris update): timers count down, after 2.0 s of the 6.0 the piece switches collision mode, at 0 the piece is destroyed; the barricade object is destroyed when all 10 slots are done [C].
- `IOB_Etat_Init`: if the level already recorded this barricade as destroyed (`fn@0x440500(self,0,0xc9,0x5dc)` id + `fn@0x432f10(G+0xc08, id)`), all pieces are removed immediately (persistence across checkpoints) [C]. `this+0x238/0x23c` (halved at init) are the size of the navigation tag that blocks the network until destroyed (`fn@0x427b90` +0x10 / -0x10).
- `Interactive_Bambou` (`ib_*`) is NOT a barricade: it is the throwable bamboo pickup (weapon ids 6/7/8, variant `this+0xc4`), see JACK_SPEARS.
