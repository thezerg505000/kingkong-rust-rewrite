## Behaviour
While Kong holds Ann the state is `k_ETAT_grab_ANN` (0x15f). Speed is gated by the analogue value: anim 0xd4/0xd5 (walk) from stick >= 0.1, run-like anim at >= 0.6, a ray of 5.0 ahead checks obstacles; the hold counter `Kong+0x80` is cleared on entry. It leaves to Kong's main state when damage is detected (`k_exec_detect_paf`). Jumps, swings and walls are restricted because those states are reached only from `k_ETAT_main`/`k_ETAT_jump`, which are not entered from 0x15f.
