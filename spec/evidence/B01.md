# B01 Jack versus V-Rex (encounter rules as a whole, level 03E "Chased by the T-Rex")
Main doc: `X04.md` (the rex), `G17.md` (guns), `A12.md` (bait), `A17.md` (weights). Evidence level: code `[C]`, level data `fx/rex_encounter_03e.json` (stream `ff001b17`).

## Behaviour (what the game does)
The 03E encounter is a **mix of scripted cinematics and the free AI**. The rex GAO (key `0x08001b4f`) is referenced by ~170 `TrigCINE_*` records. A cinematic trigger sends the rex an AI message (`exec_check_msg@0x869fa0`) that overrides the free loop unless the rex is in states 3, 6, 8, 0xc, 0xe, 0xf, 0x10, 0x11, 0x13 (down, dead, holding, falling, flinch, fade, burn): 0x186a1 -> CINE_WAIT, 0x186a2 -> CINE_VALA (go to a point), 0x186a5 -> CINE_WAITDEFAULT, 0x186a8 -> CINE_FIGHT, 0x186ab -> CINE_ROAR (`TrigCINE_Roar@0x766390` sends it), 0x186ac -> MORD (scripted bite), 0x186a4 -> end of cinematic (back to ATTENTE). While a scripted fight or bite is running the actor is flagged 0x8000 and is the forced target of rule 1. Between cinematics the rex runs the free AI of `X04.md` inside its **territory** (7 `NET_TRex` waypoint groups, first waypoints `[0,5,16,17,31,41,45]`, per-waypoint weights `af_way_ponderation`): it only targets actors whose territory is in its mask (Jack only with `+0x3d0`).
Rules for the player, all `[C]` unless marked:
* **Evade**: the rex walks (2.2 m/s) until it has Jack as target and then runs (14 m/s, 17 with Jack attached) but speeds up only 1.5 m/s per s, so Jack gets a head start of tens of metres (distance to reach 14 m/s from 2.2: ~64 m [L arithmetic]); Jack runs 4.5 m/s.
* **Hide**: Jack in script mode 2 with the hide sub-flag (`+0xb60`) is skipped by `check_vision`; the level markers `LD_Activate_Script_Trex_Cache`, `LD_03E_Activate_Cache_TRex02` ("cache") are the hide spots [L]. Hiding does not stop noise or shot flags.
* **Distract**: bait = any corpse (A12). Calm Jack loses to the nearest corpse; a Jack who has shot it (> 10 damage) or hit it once for >= 20 outranks bait. Gunshots are heard within 150 m.
* **Escape**: checkpoints `LD_PosTrex_CHK01..03` / `LD_Pos_Jack_CHK01..03` reposition both actors; the rex cannot be killed, the encounter ends by script.
* **Lethality**: a bite wounds a healthy Jack (flag 0x4104, longest wound timer 15.5 / 13.5 / 10.5 s by difficulty); a bite on a wounded Jack, or being within 2.5 m, is a grab that ends in a paf with flags 0x4a10 = death; there is no contact damage (`check_collision` is empty).
* **Protect companions**: no code in the rex for companions in 03E (classes 2..12 are never chosen in the narrow cone; they only enter via the weighted loop with LOS or via script).
* Audio/camera: footstep shake amp 0.025*k (flag 1) / 0.05*k (flag 2), k = 1 - max(d-10,0)/190, d <= 200 m [C, from `rex_encounter_03e.json`]; roar shake `fn@0x7d48e0(0.01)` within 25 m; one `TrigExec_ShakeCamAndRumble` instance (amp 0.075/30 Hz, 0.15/20 Hz, decay 1.02) is an environment event, not tied to the rex.
