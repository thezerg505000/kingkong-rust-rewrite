# A08 Attack animation (raptor view; one species)
Main doc: `spec/evidence/X02.md`. The engine class `PNJ_Raptor_*` serves species 0xe (raptor), 0x10 (Jack-level V-Rex), 0x16 (compies); this is how the **raptor** (0xe) does it.

## Behaviour (what the game does)
`ETAT_MORD` (7) plays anim 0x46 or 0x4c (alternating via `+0x377c = (n+1)%2`); the hit is armed during frames 14..19 of 0x46 (and from frame 0x1a of 0x4c) and is a single test per bite. Bite sound 7. Damage to Jack: 6 (variant 0/1) / 10 (alpha), wound class heavy (flag 4) for 0 and 2, medium (flag 2) for variant 1. A raptor's first landed bite on Jack is a grab (counter `+0x1d64`, never reset in raptor code; GRAB state, Jack anims 0x5a/0x5b, kill message 0x204 after 4.0 s hold). The leap anims 0x38-0x3b belong to traversal (VIRTUEL_saut), 0x3c/0x45 to the hidden-place ambush.
