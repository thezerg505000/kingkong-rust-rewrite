# H04 Critical damage
## Behaviour
A hit while status==1 (wounded) kills, provided the post-hit cooldown (3-5 s) is over. A hit during the cooldown is swallowed. Flag 0x200 (rex grab) kills regardless. Recovering (2) is not critical: the next hit only wounds again.
