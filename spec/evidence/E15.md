# E15 Dynamic cover
## Behaviour
Jack's cover behaviour is the state `H_ETAT_IA_MultiCover@0x5d5000` (13 kB, 43 callees) used by the multi-cover companion/Jack AI: it chooses a position to hide behind from candidate points using squared-distance gates (25 = 5 m, 36 = 6 m, 64 = 8 m, 100 = 10 m, 6.25 = 2.5 m, 4.0) and timers (3.0, 5.0 s), and `H_ETAT_IA_hide` is the player hide state. The engine has no "cover object" class: cover is the geometry plus the creature perception rules (line of sight rays in each species' `check_vision`, and noise/visual cones in E-docs of creatures). Creatures also refuse to path through nav-tagged zones (`fn@0x427b90` tags).
## Code
`H_ETAT_IA_MultiCover@0x5d5000`, `H_ETAT_IA_hide`, `fn@0x427b90` (tag), `PNJ_Raptor_exec_check_vision` (LOS), `Raycast_Object`.
## Numbers
Distance gates (squared) 25/36/64/100/6.25, timers 3.0/5.0 s seen in MultiCover [C that they occur; role [L]].
