# A14 Interspecies combat (V-Rex view)
Main doc: `X04.md`; shared candidate machinery in `X02.md`.

## Behaviour (what the game does)
The rex treats every non-human perceived actor as prey with no line-of-sight requirement. A bite on any class outside 1..12 (Venatosaurus 0xe, compies 0x16, scolo 0x1a, bats 0x14, spiders 0x20...) is **always a grab** (`exec_bite@0x84cae0`, `local_48 = 1`): `ETAT_GRAB` plays the victim's death animation 0x5b (`ANIM_Play(victim, 0x5b)`) and releases it with `fn@0x7dcd40(link, 2)`; the rex needs only the usual bite cone (3.8 m reach). It reaches such prey in two ways: narrow-cone rule 7 (after 4 s on the current target) and the weighted loop, where creature beacons weigh 150 against Jack's 100, so an alerted rex prefers a raptor inside its territory to a Jack who is not shooting. Prey is chased at **walk speed**; only Jack (or an attached target) gets the run gait (`+0x1a58`). Dead victims become corpses (A12). The rex never loses hp or flinches from creatures: only paf flags 0x200/0x100/0x80 move its hp. [C]
Nothing in `PNJ_Raptor_*` makes the raptor flee from the rex or fight it by itself; the raptor treats the rex beacon (class 0x10, weight 150, always seen) as an ordinary candidate (rule 7 / weighted loop) [L]. The `PNJ_Bontosaure_Patte` (brontosaurus foot) is special-cased as an attacker in `check_paf` (skipped unless the receiver is in states 3, 0xe, 0xf, 0x10, 0x13) [C, X02].

## Code
`exec_bite@0x84cae0`, `ETAT_GRAB@0x847af0`, `exec_update_best_interest@0x863360`, `fn@0x47e180`, `exec_test_bite@0x866ed0` (class-specific target bones: 0x14 -> bone 2, 0x1a -> bone 5, 0xe -> bone 0 or 0x13).

## Numbers
bite reach 3.8 m (2.5 in FIGHT), cone cos 0.766 [C]; victim anim 0x5b [C]; creature weight 150 vs Jack 100 [C/L]; walk 2.2 m/s vs run 14 m/s [L].

## Rust
`vrex_jack::bite_outcome` (non-human -> `Grab`), `select_target` weighted loop; test `rex_prefers_raptors_to_jack_by_interest`, `bite_is_a_strike_on_a_healthy_jack_and_a_grab_on_a_wounded_one`.

## Gaps
Whether any level places raptors inside the rex's territory (03E has one rex territory set, no raptors found in `fx/rex_encounter_03e.json`); the raptor-side reaction to a rex (flee/ignore) is not coded in `PNJ_Raptor_*` and may live in level scripts; Kong-level creatures vs the KT rex are not covered.
