# K04 Jumping (main document for K04, K05)
Tags: `[C]` visible in the decompiled C, `[L]` inferred, `[G]` guess. Offsets are `Kong+0xNNN`. Static analysis of KingKong8.exe only.

## Behaviour
Jumps are hotspot driven. `k_exec_check_hotspot` scans the level's hotspot objects and keeps the ones inside a height/distance window in front of Kong (dot > 0.5); the best one inside a 20 m cone is stored at `Kong+0xa18`. `k_ETAT_jump` (state 0x3e9) then launches Kong toward it with a ballistic solver (`Ballistic_SolveLaunchVelocity@0x56a7c0`). Without a hotspot the jump reach is 6 m standing, 10 m with the stick active, 16 m with the hold button; release states (swing, wall leap, column) override the reach once through `Kong+0x9bc`. Anims: 0x3c standing, 0x3d running; landings 0x3a/0x3f/0x41, fall loop 0x40.
