# X09 Giant spiders (`PNJ_Spider` Jack side, `PNJ_KSpider` Kong side)
Status RESEARCHED.

## Behaviour
Jack side `PNJ_Spider_*` (18 functions `@0x4edc20-0x4f7b11`): sits in a lair (`Attente`), reacts to proximity/stimuli (`check_vision`, `check_best_interet`), drops/pounces on prey (`Attaque`, flags `0x48009004` dynamics, jumping `test_jump`), crawls on ground (`Sol`) and snaps (`Snap`); `callback_WhenSectoOn` activates it when the sector loads. "Food bait and fire" responses: the spider scores interested objects through `check_best_interet` with a 4 s memory (`Time_Elapsed(+0x87, 4.0)`) and class-based interest; fire is a paf type (the `check_paf` hit reaction ranges 1..10 scaled by a hp field `+0x194`). Kong side `PNJ_KSpider_*` (9 functions, `@0x548b80-0x54b5b0`, plus `exec_interaction_add_data/init`): a simpler ambush creature (`Apparition`, `Attente`, `Attaque`, `Disparition`) that Ann reacts to (`KAnn_exec_action_interaction_attack_spider`).
