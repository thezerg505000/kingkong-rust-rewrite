# I06 Recover thrown spear
## Behaviour (what the game does)
A thrown spear that embedded in terrain or a creature stays in the world (moving with its host) and can be picked up within 3 m; it returns with its wear counter and its lit flag; a spent spear can be picked up but cannot embed again. Spears farther than 15 m are removed after 3 s (not the newest), and the world keeps at most 32.
