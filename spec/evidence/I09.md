# I09 Companion supply (Hayes)
## Behaviour
Hayes (slot 3) has his own weapons and ammo in the same arrays as Jack's (slot-indexed `G+0x342c+0x48*w+4*slot` clip, `G+0x39d4+...` reserve, held weapon `G+0x3344+4*slot`). The supply mechanic is an exchange: the companion's "psss" state (`H_ETAT_IA_psss@0x5d88a0`, id 0x25e) swaps weapons with Jack. `fn@0x53a760` picks the pair (companion's weapon = its held firearm (1..4) else holstered else stashed; Jack's likewise), and `Jack_ExchangeWeaponSlots@0x53a8e0` performs it with the ammo: the companion's weapon type and its clip/reserve go to Jack, Jack's weapon type and clip/reserve go to the companion, both old entries are zeroed first. Non-firearm items (grenade 5, javelin 6, 7, 8, 9, lever 10) are handed over by spawning a thrown object (`DAT_00b99298` grenade, `DAT_00b99374` javelin, ...) that flies to Jack ("psss, catch"), and the item cells are rotated (`G+0x3348/0x3394/0x33e0`, `G+0x4520/0x4524`). Spoken lines per slot: see N02.md. The order 4 (`H_ETAT_jimmy_munitions@0x5dffb0`, id 0x3f2) is the companion walking to Jack with the ammo order: it approaches within the same loop (1.0 s accumulation `this+0xc18`, then `fn@0x7dd3f0(7)` closes the order) [C for structure, ammo amount per hand-over L: the transfer itself is the exchange above].
`TrigCINE_ResetMunitionHayes@0x7665d0` is a script action that zeroes slot 3's clip and reserve for weapons 1..4 (offsets `G+0x3480/0x34c8/0x3510/0x3558` and `0x3a28/0x3a70/0x3ab8/0x3b00`) [C]: e.g. after a cutscene where Hayes gave away or spent everything.
