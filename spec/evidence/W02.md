# W02 Water surfaces
## Behaviour
Water is ground material in the same grid as fire. Materials 3, 9, 10, 11, 12 put Jack in "wade mode" (`H_IsWadeMode@0x689ff0`, 13 callers: movement, sounds, ramasse/pick-up, hide, paf, throw states), which changes locomotion and footstep sound. Material 3 is the swamp crawler's water (`SC_compute_NearestCaseOfWater@0x551210` searches material-3 cells; `SC_ETAT_gotowater/nage/waitinwater`). Thrown spears do not stick in materials 1, 3, 5, 9, 10, 11, 12 (`Javelin_waittaken@0x87d160`, unless the hit object has flag 0x400000). The visual water plane effect is `MM_Exec_Water_Height@0x644070`: effect amplitude `this+0xe50` is full while the camera is within 50 units of the plane height `this+0xe44` and fades linearly to zero at 200 units. `TrigExec_WaterEnable@0x78c1e0` lets a trigger enable a water volume (`fn@0x41f090`). Water also stops grid fire because only material 8 burns.
## Code
`H_IsWadeMode`, `MM_Exec_Water_Height`, `TrigExec_WaterEnable`, `SC_*` (swamp crawler), `Javelin_waittaken`, `PNJ_EatMe_ETAT_Water@0x52a830` (bait creature's water state), `fn@0x68a930@0x68a930` (ground 1 / 0xc / burning check).
