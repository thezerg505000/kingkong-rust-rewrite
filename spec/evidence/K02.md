# K02 Camera modes
Tags: `[C]` visible in the decompiled C, `[L]` inferred, `[G]` guess. Offsets are `Kong+0xNNN`. Static analysis of KingKong8.exe only.

## Behaviour
`CM_Kong_Modes` runs every frame for the Kong levels. It first tries `CM_Kong_AutoMode`, then `CM_Kong_FightSelector`; if neither takes over it chooses, in order, Cine, Cheat, Test, Finish, CChamp (timed override), Cut (scripted cut camera) and finally Normal. Normal mode follows Kong with smoothed blends (rate 1.0 per second, clamped) and height 3.8; Walling and Swing-pillar have their own modes. The camera is independent of Kong's heading: Kong's facing is derived from the stick through the camera axes (K01), not the reverse.
