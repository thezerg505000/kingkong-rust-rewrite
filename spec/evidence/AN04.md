# AN04 Ann vulnerability
Main doc for the Ann struct: `AN01.md`.

## Behaviour
Ann has no hit points. Any hit that the receiver accepts sends her to `KAnn_ETAT_paf` (state 5, hurt clip 0xad, speech request); when that clip ends she goes to `KAnn_ETAT_mort` (state 6, clip 0x3a) and stays dead; the engine also writes 30.0 into `Univers+0x4dd4` each frame in both states (a timer the game-over/restart logic reads [L]). So "exposed to enemy attacks" = one accepted hit kills her. Hits are accepted unless she is flagged invulnerable (`Ann+0xfe8`) or `Univers+0x8904` is non-zero; a hit word containing bit 0x80 is always accepted (scripted/cinematic kill). The V-Rex jaw word 0x20000 kills her straight from `grabbed_trex`. The rest of the world is frozen while she dies: `KAnn_ETAT_paf` sets flag 1 (`fn@0x415490(actor, 1)`) on every actor except Ann and the killer when `Ann+0x1090` (source) is set [C], a short kill-cam freeze [L].
"Protected": she is protected while carried only in the sense that Kong's hand state does not make her immune: `grabbed_KK` calls `KAnn_exec_CHECK_paf` too, and a hit sends her to `paf` (`b991d0`) even from Kong's hand [C].

## Code
* `KAnn_exec_CHECK_paf@0x5f71b0`: clears `+0x230`; iterates the type-4 messages addressed to her; for each, `acc = fn@0x51f190(msg)` (hit flag word); accepted if `fn@0x881140(Ann)` (= `!Ann+0xfe8 && MATH_IsZero(Univers+0x8904)`, `fn@0x8810c0@0x8810c0` reads `+0xfe8`) or `acc & 0x80`; then `+0x230 |= acc`, `+0x234..0x23c = hit position`, `+0x22c = sender`, prints `"KANN prend un paf de "`, and `fn@0x5ee140(3)` (sets `Ann+4 = 3` and the per-player flag `Univers2+0x1bb4+4*player = 3`) [C].
* `KAnn_exec_CHECK_paf_javelin@0x6018f0`: same collection without the acceptance test, used by `TRACK_Reflex` only while holding a javelin [C].
* `KAnn_ETAT_follow_network`, `action`, `grabbed_KK`, `grabbed_bat`: after CHECK_paf, `if (Ann+0x22c != 0)` -> `Ann+4 = 3` and goto `paf` (`DAT_b991d0`) [C].
* `KAnn_ETAT_paf@0x5f7370`: anim 0xad, speech (`fn@0x51ef50(.., 0xd0006f22, ..)`), freeze others, exit -> `mort` when `fn@0x424940` (anim ended) [C].
* `KAnn_ETAT_grabbed_trex@0x5fd090`: after 0 < state timer, `CHECK_paf`; hit word with 0x20000 -> `mort`, else back to the paf/mort chain [C].
* Damage packets that reach her: Kong-level rex `KT_exec_zdf_zdc` (flags 0x20000 vs Ann, B02.md), raptors `KR_exec_bite` (10 per bite on Kong; vs Ann see X02), spider `PNJ_KSpider_ETAT_Attaque` flags 0x20 damage 1 (X09), bats `KBC_*` grab (AN09).
