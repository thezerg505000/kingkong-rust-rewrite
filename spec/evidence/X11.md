# X11 Flying bats / Terapusmordax (`PNJ_Corbeau`, `BC_*`, `KBC_*`)
Status RESEARCHED.

## Behaviour
Three bat families. (a) Jack-level crows/small flyers `PNJ_Corbeau_*` (8 functions `@0x5038e0-0x505b7f`): `attente`, `envol` (take-off), `follow_network`, banking and morph: scenery flocks with random banking (+/-60 degrees, +/-90 degrees) and scale/speed randomness; no attack. (b) `BC_*` (70 functions `@0x6c6280-0x74aca5`, `PNJ_BatsCharognards@0x7291e0`): Jack-level carrion bats: `Move_*` (network flight, take-off/land, perch), `Fight_Approche/Contact/Pause`, `Paf`, `Mange`, `Sol_*`, cadaver eating (`Move_Cadavre`). (c) `KBC_*` (76 functions `@0x6ab590-0x7d05a5`, `PNJ_KBatsCharognards@0x7aa4a0`, manager `KBM_*@0x4c69d0-0x4c7540`): the Kong-level big bats that swoop at Kong, grab Ann (`KBC_ETAT_Sol_Interaction_Ann`, AN09), ride Kong (`KK_Ride`, `KK_Grabbed`, `KK_Finish`, `KK_Projectile`), fall (`Chute_Libre`), die (`Sol_Mort/Agonie/Ecrasement/Fade`), wait in caves (`Wait_Grotte`, `Wait_Perchee`). Bats attack in groups and the dead provide meat for predators (corpse beacons `KCadavre_Wait`, weight 150).
