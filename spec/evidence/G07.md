# G07 Ammunition reporting
## Behaviour
When Jack's magazine falls to a per-weapon threshold, nobody has spoken recently and the call-out has not already fired, Jack says an ammo line; reserve empty gives the "out of ammo" line (min interval 1 s), otherwise "low" (60 s). The flag resets when the magazine goes above the threshold. Hayes has a similar no-ammo line (90 s).
