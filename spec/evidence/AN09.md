# AN09 Scripted capture (enemies can abduct Ann)
Main doc: `AN01.md`. Bats: `X11.md`, rex: `B02.md`.

## Behaviour
Two abductors exist in code: the big bat (`KBC_*`, `KAnn_ETAT_grabbed_bat`, Ann state 7) and the V-Rex (`KT_*`, `KAnn_ETAT_grabbed_trex`, state 0xd).
* **Bat**: a level trigger commands the abduction: `TrigExec_KBigBat_Grab_Ann` writes the bat handle into `Univers+0x8998` and (if its `+0x54` parameter is non-zero) `Univers+0x899c = 1`. `TrigTest_KBigBat_IsGrabbingAnn` tests that a bat object holds Ann (`bat+0x1c` set and `bat+0x414` link valid and its `+0x408 == +0xcf4`). When the link exists Ann enters `grabbed_bat`: she is parented to the bat's bone 0xa0 (`fn@0x4266c0(bat, 0xa0)`), her collision hidden, anims 0x7d (carried) / 0x7e (struggle) / 0x7f,0x80 (kicking) / 0x82; struggle speech repeats after `Rand_Range(8, 15)` s. The bat later lands (`KBC_ETAT_Sol_Interaction_Ann`, KBC state 9, clip 0x5a, "BIG BAT Wait for Eat") and eats her unless Kong intervenes (bat flag bits 1 and 0x10 on the bat end the state).
* **Rex**: `propose_grab_trex` (AN01) offers her after a 10 s timer within 4 m; `grabbed_trex` plays clip 0x3a, parents her to bone-handle `Ann+0x14c`, and a hit word with 0x20000 kills her. The Kong-level rex picks Kong or Ann via `KT_exec_fight_actor_select` (B02.md).
Abduction of Ann by the raptor (`KR_ETAT_interact_ANN`) exists as an interaction state (no grab) [C name only].

## Code / Numbers
`KAnn_ETAT_grabbed_bat@0x5fa9a0`: entry writes `Ann+0x218 = Ann+0x144` (the bat), `+0x108 = 1`, `+0xfc = 1`, `+0x228 = (clock >= 1.0)`; copies the Ann position `+0x21c..0x224`; hit while held -> `+0x1090 = sender`, `Ann+4 = 3`, goto `paf`. `KBC_ETAT_Sol_Interaction_Ann@0x7bcee0`. `TrigTest_KBigBat_PlungeonAttack` tests the bat's attack phase (`bat+0xce0 == 0x12 / 0x14` with a parameter) [C]; `TrigExec_KBigBat_Attack` sets `param+0x68 = 7` and sends the attack message.

## Rust
`AnnState::GrabbedBat/GrabbedTrex`, `BAT_STRUGGLE_SPEECH`, `next_state_after_hit`.

## Gaps
The bat's decision to abduct (KBC flight AI) is script-driven (`TrigExec_KBigBat_Grab_Ann`); the timing from grab to eat and the exact bat state numbers of `KBC_ETAT_*` are in X11.md.
