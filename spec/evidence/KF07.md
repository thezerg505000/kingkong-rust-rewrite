# KF07 Fury termination
## Behaviour (what the game does)
Fury is a countdown (`Kong+0x7c4`, seconds). It runs down in real time except while Kong is in a
grab/finish struggle (actions 0x160, 0x352, 0x353, 0x15e). It reaches 0 on its own, or is shortened by hits
taken (2.5/5.0 s), extended by landed blows (+1.5 s up to 15 s), or cleared at once when Kong dies or a
cinematic starts. At 0 the meter and flag are zeroed and the FX fade out at 0.2/s.
## Code
`k_exec_fury@0x8b1d00`, `k_ETAT_paf@0x8c24a0`, `k_reflex@0x8a4360`, `k_ETAT_finished@0x8b5c80`.
