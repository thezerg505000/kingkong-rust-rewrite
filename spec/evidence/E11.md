# E11 Multi-operator doors
## Behaviour
A two-pillar door opens only while **every** pillar has an operator and every operator is in turning position. Operators are Jack (pillar flag 0x80) and companions (pillar flag 0x100). If only some pillars are operated the door is *held* (neither opens nor closes, flags 0x600), so partial effort is not lost; when nobody operates it falls back to the closing rule; when all operators are ready the pillars are released for rotation (flag 0x4000) and the door opens at `1/openTime` per second. Companions are chosen automatically: for each pillar `fn@0x48db90` ranks candidate companions (active slots 2..6) by squared distance to the pillar (multiplied by 4 for Ann, 5 for Denham, 2 for slot 6 when door flag `this+0x530` is set, else equal) with insertion sort, keeps up to 10, and `fn@0x48df40` stores up to 4 of them in the lever's list (`lever+0x230+4k`, count `lever+0x228`) and hands each companion the crank rate 0.576 (`+0x3448`). The first free candidate walks to the pillar's stand point (0.71 behind, 0.5 aside, mirrored if the pillar is flagged 0x20), sets anim 0x66 (turn) when the pillar is neither held (0x400) nor blocked (0x800) and anim 0x65 (waiting) otherwise; a waiting non-Ann companion complains with speech 0x4c after 5.0 s. Waiting count/speech cues: 1 = "need the other lever/operator" (sound 0x35, 0x36 when the door has two pillars), 2 = 0x34, 3 = 0x39, 4 = 0x38 (nobody near, uses the nearest active slot), 5 = Denham line, 6/7 = a companion tells Jack (0x13/0x38), 8 = 0x4c [C].
## Code
`InteractiveDoor_loop@0x876330` (per-pillar flags: 0x80 player operating, 0x100 companion operating, 0x400 held, 0x800 other pillar not ready, 0x1000 operator in position, 0x2000 allow, 0x4000 rotate; counters `this[0x104]` levers mounted, `this[0x105]` operated), `H_ETAT_IA_use@0x5d2710` (kind 5), `H_ETAT_Ia_porte@0x5e1b60` (human), `fn@0x48db90`, `fn@0x48df40`, `fn@0x48da30`, `InteractiveDoor_exec_speech`.
## Numbers
rank multipliers 4/5/2/1, up to 10 ranked and 4 stored; speech cooldown 30 s per cue; speaker within 10 m [C].
## Rust
`companions::{pillar_gate, Gate, Operator, PillarState, rank_helpers, pick_door_speaker, door_speech_sound}`; tests `two_pillar_door_needs_both_operators_ready`, `helpers_are_ranked_by_weighted_distance`, `door_speaker_and_sounds`.
## Gaps
Transitions of the pillar flags 0x800 / 0x1000 are set in two places (door loop and operator states) and only their end effect is modelled; the order in which several companions fill the list is by rank, but a companion already busy elsewhere is not skipped in the model.
