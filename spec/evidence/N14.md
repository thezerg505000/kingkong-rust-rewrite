# N14 Cinematic events
## Behaviour
NPCs take part in scripted sequences through a per-human message queue (`H_exec_ch_msg@0x5b3d70`, capacity 20: "Trop de messages cines humains"). Trigger actions send: 0x186a1 Wait (`TrigCINE_Wait`), 0x186a2 Vala (move along a waypoint path, `TrigCINE_Vala`/`H_CINE_Vala`), 0x186a3 Speech (`TrigCINE_Speech`), 0x186a4 clear/resume (restores saved follow state `this+0xb08..0xb14`), 0x186a5 WaitDefault, 0x186a6 ResetWaitDefault, 0x186a7 ForceEtat (parameter table, below), 0x186a8. The queue is only consumed while the human is in the follow / hide / assist / psss / multi-cover states, so an NPC finishing its current job first is how scripts "do not always interrupt" control. Cinematic states: `H_CINE_Wait`, `H_CINE_WaitDefault`, `H_CINE_Vala`, `H_CINE_Speech`, `H_CINE_Shoot@0x5e1240`.
FORCE_ETAT sub-codes (value in `param`): 0 `+0xaa4`, 1 `+0xaa8`, 2 `+0x2f28`, 3 `+0xaac`, 4 `+0xb54`, 5 give item (10 = lever, >100: weapon*100 + flag, else held weapon id), 6 `+0xab0`, 7 `+0xab4`, 8 `+0xab8`, 9 `+0x3b60` (shoot flinch %), 0xa `+0x3b7c`, 0xb `+0xabc`, 0xc status 3 (dead), 0xd..0x18 `+0xac0..0xae0` flags, 0xe `+0x3cbc`, 0xf `+0x3cc0`, 0x11 sets `G2+0x16934` and `+0x3d1c` (global cine flag + 10 s timer), 0x19 `+0x41d0` float, 0x1a `+0x41e0/0x41e4` [C].
## Rust
`companions::{cine, force_etat_give_item}`.
## Gaps
Meaning of most `+0xaa..` flags unnamed (model names are unreliable at those offsets).
