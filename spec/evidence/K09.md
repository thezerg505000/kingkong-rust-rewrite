Tags: `[C]` visible in the decompiled C, `[L]` inferred, `[G]` guess. Offsets are `Kong+0xNNN`. Static analysis of KingKong8.exe only.

## Behaviour
Two swing states. SwingArch (0x3b6, anims 0x8f grab / 0x90 swing) and SwingPillar (0x3b7, anims 0x91/0x92, mode camera 4 "swing pillar"). Kong must have the jump flag; both run `k_exec_joy` each frame and apply a pendulum speed through Dyn_SetSpeed. Releasing (button 2 = jump, or animation end) writes a one-shot jump reach into `Kong+0x9bc` and hands over to `k_ETAT_jump`: arch 8.0; pillar 10.0 normally, 13.0 (with Kong+0xa00 = 1.5, Kong+0x9c4 = 2.5) for the strong release and 5.0 for a short one. Entry applies velocity (0, -0.5, 2.4) with speed scale 2.8 (shared with the wall-hang).
