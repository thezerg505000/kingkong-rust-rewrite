# KC12 Throw finisher
## Behaviour (what the game does)
Besides the jaw-break, Kong can grab the rex (message kind 1 grab, mask bit 1; accepted in any state, and in KO)
and finish with a **throw** or a **ground slam**. In `KT_ETAT_grabbed` (id 0x65): throw messages 0x17/0x21 play the
rex's anim 0x7a and it flies (`projectile`, 0x14); landing/hitting a surface deals 20.0 damage, velocity x0.6
bounce, and life 0 -> `KO_au_sol` (`Rex+0x2e0 = 5.0`, or 2.0 if the throw anim ended at life 0). Ground slams
(messages 0x16/0x18/0x22/0x23; rex anims 0x7b, 0x8a, 0x8d, 0x9e, 0xa1 by move id 0x11c/0x11e/0x126/0x128) at
life 0 end in `mort` (death) when the anim is 0x8d or 0xa1.
## Code
`KT_ETAT_grabbed@0x4abd50` (grab move protocol, ends via `fn@0x770af0(life, 20.0)` at the throw impact and at
the end of the throw anim), `KT_ETAT_projectile@0x4a1fa0` (flight, impact 20.0, derap/KO/mort exits), `KT_exec_zdf_zdc`
(mode 4/8 packet when the flung rex hits Kong: flags 0x41, damage 20), `fn@0x559fd0@0x559fd0` (death notice to Kong
`fn@0x52f950(kong, 0xf)`).
## Numbers
Impact damage 20.0 [C]; bounce 0.6 [C]; KO 5.0 s / 2.0 s [C]; flung-rex-hits-Kong 20 dmg flags 0x41 [C]; ends: anim 0x7a at life 0 ->
KO, anim 0x8d/0xa1 at life 0 -> mort, other end anims -> projectile [C].
## Data
Kong's grab-move ids (0x6f, 0xf1, 0xf2, 0x64-0x68, 0xf5/0xf6, 0x119-0x128) are Kong-side move numbers
(`fn@0x6e5230(msg)`), with animation names in the Kong bank.
## Rust
`kong::vrex::{grab_end_state, GrabEnd, IMPACT_DAMAGE}`; the machine routes `Grabbed -> KoAuSol|Mort|FightKong|Projectile`.
## Gaps
Which Kong input selects throw vs slam vs jaw-break is on Kong's side (`k_ETAT_grab@0x8dae10`, not audited); the
mapping of "throw finisher" (ledger) to anim 0x7a vs the slams is `[L]`.
