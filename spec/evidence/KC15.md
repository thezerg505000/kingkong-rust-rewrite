# KC15 Creature weapon
Main documents: `spec/evidence/KC14.md` (wield), `spec/evidence/KC06.md` (grab protocol).

## Behaviour (what the game does)
Creatures use the same holding machinery: a grabbed creature whose link kind carries bit 4 is wielded (`Kong+0x378 = 1`) and `k_exec_melee_hit` takes its
"holding" branch: the victim is hit with the *held creature as the hand* (`fn@0x42edb0(held, 6)` position), damage 10 (20 fury), class 2
(10 for the overhead smash anim 0xc6; `Kong+0x112c == 0xc6`), and the use counter of the link is incremented when the victim answers the life message 0x10.
`k_exec_zdf_zdc` uses zone 1 instead of 6 when the held thing is of class `b99fa4`. The held creature suffers: thrown/released raptors take 5.0 per impact (KC07).
Which creatures are wieldable (kind bit 4) is decided on the creature side (`*_exec_propose_grab` of KR, KBC, BC, Scolo, crab): not audited.
